  3. US trading days at 09:50, 11:30, 13:30 and 15:57 New York: the SPY opening-range paper day trade reports in the chat.

  5. Weekdays at 16:41 New York: the Strategy Lab's daily run and paper trial log.
How to read this page

Sharpe is return divided by its ups and downs, per year; above 1 is strong, below 0.5 is weak. The t-stat says how unlikely a result is by luck; with about 2,930 rules tried, luck alone produces a best t near 3.5, so the bar is 4.19. "Weak" means real but small or fragile: worth a live paper test, not real money. Research and practice, not financial advice.
